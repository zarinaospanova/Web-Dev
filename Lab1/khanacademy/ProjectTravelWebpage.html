Travel to USA

The United States is a large and diverse country in North America.
It offers big cities, national parks, beaches, deserts, and cultural diversity.

Basic facts:

  • language: English
  • Currency: US Dollar (USD)
  • Transport: cars,planes,trains
  • Climate: varies by state and season
  • Visa: ESTA or tourist visa required for most travelers

Top 5 Popular States for Tourists

  1. New York
    • Famous for New York City
    • Attractions: Times Square, Central Park, Statue of Liberty
    • Best for: culture, museums, shopping, city life
  2. California
    • Attractions: Los Angeles, Hollywood, San Francisco, beaches
    • Also, famous for national parks (Yosemite)
    • Best for: ocean views, movies, technology, nature
  3. Florida
    • Known for warm weather and beaches
    • Attractions: Miami, Orlando, Disney World
    • Best for: family trips, beaches, theme parks
  4. Texas
    • One of the largest states
    • Cities: Houston, Dallas, Austin
    • Best for: culture, food (BBQ), music, history
  5. Nevada
    • Famous for Las Vegas
    • Attractions: casinos, shows, nightlife
    • Best for: entertainment and desert landscapes
