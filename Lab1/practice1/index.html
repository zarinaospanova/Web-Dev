Kendall Jenner

Kendall Jenner is an American fashion model and media personality. Born on November 3, 1995, she rose to fame through the reality TV series Keeping Up with the Kardashians, which followed the lives of the Kardashian–Jenner family.
Kendall is best known for her work in high fashion. She has walked the runway for top designers and fashion houses such as Chanel, Versace, and Givenchy, and has appeared on the covers of major magazines including Vogue and Harper’s Bazaar. Unlike many models who start in traditional modeling agencies, Kendall successfully transitioned from reality television to the elite fashion world.
Beyond modeling, she is involved in business ventures, brand endorsements, and social media influencing. Kendall is also known for her minimalist style, reserved personality compared to her famous sisters, and her advocacy for mental health awareness.
